What ChatDFT can do

Type what you want in plain language. The planner reads the request, picks a molecule and a job type, and runs it with PySCF.

The planner is a language model when one is configured — the pill at the top right names it — and a built-in keyword parser otherwise. Either way every answer says which of the two read your sentence, and if the model could not be reached the reply says why instead of quietly falling back.

  • Single point — energy, orbitals, dipole, charges.
  • Optimisation — relax the geometry (geomeTRIC).
  • Excited states — TD-DFT (TDA) and a UV-Vis curve.
  • Vibrations — harmonic frequencies, IR spectrum and thermochemistry.
  • DOS — total and element-projected density of states.
  • Reactivity — conceptual DFT descriptors and condensed Fukui functions.
  • Surfaces — MEP map, density and orbital isosurfaces.
  • NCI — reduced density gradient: hydrogen bonds, dispersion contacts and steric clashes.
  • Scan — a rigid bond scan, giving a potential-energy curve.

Name a molecule from the library, give a SMILES, or paste a formula. Add “in water” for an implicit solvent, or “with charge -1” for an ion.

Each figure carries PNG and CSV buttons underneath it, so the data behind a plot can be redrawn at publication resolution.
